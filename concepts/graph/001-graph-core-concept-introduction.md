## Author Hints

- 核心觀念：Graphs model pairwise relationships between objects using vertices and edges.
- Pattern 辨識線索：Look for network-like structures, relationships, dependencies, or connections.
- Thinking：Identify what entities form the nodes and what relationships form the edges.
- Common Mistakes：Confusing general graphs with trees (forgetting about cycles and multiple parents).
- TypeScript 重點：Use custom interfaces or classes to model graph nodes and edges.
- Python 重點：Use dictionaries or custom dataclasses to represent vertices and their neighbors.
